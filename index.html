The octopus starts at rest. Open Controls to play the animation or take it apart, and Map to find your way.

Big rocks, the ships, the caves, the ruins and the landmarks are solid: swim around them, or rise above them. You can even rest on top.

The great clock tower has stopped. Find every site to recover its lost gears, then return to the tower to set it running again.

The Story

The great clock tower has stopped, and its gears are scattered across the sunken sites.

Gears recovered: 0 of 13

How it works

  • Each site you find shows “Gear 3 of 13 recovered” with the site’s name and a line of its story.
  • When you’ve found all 13, you’re told to return to the clock tower. The line under the title then points you toward it, with distance and direction.
  • Swim back to the tower and it runs again. The gears spin up, the clock face blazes, and you hear a rising chime if Sound is on. A beam of light rises from the tower and the camera pulls back to show it.
  • Until then, the tower’s gears turn very slowly and its glow is dim, so you can see it is stopped. After the ending it runs at full speed.

Progress and reward

  • Your discoveries and the finished ending are saved in your browser, so they’re still there next time.
  • Finishing the story unlocks a new colorway, Midnight pearl, a steel-blue and pearl look. It joins the Colors button.
  • Use Reset progress in Controls to start over. It also clears the unlock.

About

A self-contained interactive piece built with Three.js — one file, no install, pure browser.

Coral reefs, giant kelp, shipwrecks, bioluminescence, and a clockwork cephalopod that can come apart and return to form.

Designed for desktop; touch controls appear on mobile.
